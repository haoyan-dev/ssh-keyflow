How to use

Fields are static. Terminal handoff and outcomes are simulated. This gallery does not validate the production workflow. Click a terminal to focus: ↑/↓ scroll or select · PgUp/PgDn scroll · Esc goes back · Shift+Tab returns to the Page selector. Use Page to inspect every example.

What to judge

Can you tell what this is, what happened, and what to do next without reading the whole page? Borders, spacing, wording, and information order all change between directions.
